ChronoBite Pro v2
BCSE203E · VL2025260504802 · Slot L1

A React-based food delivery platform that goes far beyond browsing menus. ChronoBite reads your biology — circadian phase, metabolic rate, heart-rate zone — and recommends exactly the right food at the right moment in your body clock.

🕐
Circadian Engine — 7-phase body-clock (Rising → Deep Rest) with real-time meal-window recommendations tuned to your wake hour
🔥
BMR & TDEE Science — Mifflin-St Jeor formula, 5 activity multipliers, goal-adjusted calorie targets (lose / maintain / gain)
❤️
Live HR Monitor — Simulated biometric stream, 5 heart-rate zones (Resting → Peak), animated ECG strip
🤖
AI Nutrition Coach — Macro breakdowns per meal type, hydration tracker, personalised tips per circadian phase
🎙️
Full Voice & A11y — Web Speech API, ARIA live regions, keyboard navigation, high-contrast mode, adjustable font sizes
